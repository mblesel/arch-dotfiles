# Academic research

## Preferred sources

- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) — discover papers, authors, and citation trails.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) — find related work and follow citations.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) — preprints in mathematics, physics, computer science, and related fields.
- [Hugging Face Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers) — discover machine-learning papers and associated discussions.
- [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/) and [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/) — computing and engineering publications.
- [Crossref](https://search.crossref.org/) — check DOI and publication metadata.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) and institutional repositories — locate author profiles or accessible copies; verify the publication details elsewhere.

## Search and assess

- Try exact titles, author names, DOI, alternate terminology, and relevant publication-year ranges.
- Follow references and later citing work to find foundational papers, critiques, and updates.
- Read the paper itself when possible. Link to the paper or its publication record rather than only to a search-result page.
- Identify whether a work is a preprint, a peer-reviewed study, a review, or a commentary; check for a later published version.
- Treat citation counts and popularity as discovery clues, not proof that a claim is sound.
